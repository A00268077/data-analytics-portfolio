# Business Requirements Document

## Project Name

Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard

---

## Project Objective

Design and develop an interactive Power BI dashboard that enables business stakeholders to monitor retail sales performance, identify trends, analyze profitability, and support data-driven decision making.

---

## Business Goals

- Increase visibility of sales performance.
- Monitor revenue and profit.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
- Compare sales across regions.
- Support executive decision making.

---

## Target Users

- Executive Management
- Sales Managers
- Regional Managers
- Business Analysts

---

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value
- Sales Growth %
- Top Products
- Top Customers

---

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview

High-level KPIs and overall business performance.

### 2. Sales Analysis

Sales trends over time with interactive filtering.

### 3. Product Performance

Top and bottom performing products.

### 4. Customer Insights

Customer segmentation and purchasing behavior.

### 5. Regional Performance

Sales and profit by country and region.

---

## Filters

- Date
- Product Category
- Product
- Country
- Region
- Customer

---

## Expected Outcome

A professional Business Intelligence dashboard suitable for executive reporting and portfolio presentation.
