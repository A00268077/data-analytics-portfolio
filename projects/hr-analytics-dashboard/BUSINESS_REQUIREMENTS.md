# Business Requirements Document

## Project Name

HR Analytics Dashboard

---

## Project Objective

Design and develop an interactive Power BI dashboard that helps HR leaders understand workforce composition, employee attrition, compensation, job satisfaction, and retention risks.

---

## Business Goals

- Monitor employee headcount.
- Measure attrition rate.
- Identify departments and roles with high attrition.
- Analyze salary distribution.
- Understand employee satisfaction.
- Identify factors associated with employee turnover.
- Support workforce planning and retention decisions.

---

## Target Users

- HR Directors
- HR Business Partners
- Department Managers
- People Analytics Teams
- Executive Management

---

## Key Performance Indicators

- Total Employees
- Active Employees
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Age
- Average Monthly Income
- Average Years at Company
- Average Job Satisfaction

---

## Dashboard Sections

### 1. Executive Overview

High-level workforce and attrition KPIs.

### 2. Attrition Analysis

Attrition by department, job role, age, overtime, and business travel.

### 3. Employee Demographics

Gender, age, education, marital status, and department composition.

### 4. Compensation Analysis

Monthly income, salary bands, job level, and stock options.

### 5. Employee Experience

Job satisfaction, work-life balance, environment satisfaction, and engagement.

---

## Filters

- Department
- Job Role
- Gender
- Age Group
- Education
- Marital Status
- Overtime
- Business Travel

---

## Expected Outcome

A professional HR analytics dashboard that helps decision-makers identify retention risks and understand workforce trends.
