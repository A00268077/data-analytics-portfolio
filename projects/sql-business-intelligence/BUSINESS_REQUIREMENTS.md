# Business Requirements

## Business Goal

Develop a PostgreSQL-based Business Intelligence solution for a multi-market retail company.

The analysis should help management understand sales performance, profitability, customer behaviour, product performance, geographic performance, operational efficiency and growth trends.

## Core Business Questions

### Sales Performance

- What is total revenue?
- What is gross profit?
- What is gross margin?
- How many completed orders were generated?
- What is the average order value?
- How are sales changing over time?
- What is the year-over-year growth rate?
- Are there seasonal sales patterns?

### Geographic Performance

- Which markets generate the most revenue?
- Which countries perform best?
- How concentrated is geographic revenue?

### Product Performance

- Which categories generate the most revenue?
- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which products generate the highest gross profit?
- Which products have the strongest margins?

### Customer Analytics

- Who are the highest-value customers?
- How concentrated is revenue among top customers?
- Which customers are inactive?
- Which customers purchase repeatedly?
- How can customers be segmented using RFM?

### Operations

- What percentage of orders are completed, pending or cancelled?
- What is the financial value of cancelled orders?
- How well are deliveries performing?
- What is the return rate?

## Technical Requirements

The solution should demonstrate:

- PostgreSQL
- Relational database modelling
- Primary and foreign keys
- INNER and LEFT JOIN
- Aggregations
- CTEs
- Subqueries
- Window functions
- LAG and LEAD
- ROW_NUMBER, RANK and DENSE_RANK
- Running totals
- Moving averages
- Views
- Query optimization
- Indexing
- Reporting-ready datasets for Power BI
